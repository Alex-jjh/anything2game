# A/B: classic vs classic + support gate vs layered (2026-11-18/19)

**Scope**: the pre-registered experiment of [layered-generation.md §7](../architecture/layered-generation.md). Same corpus (6 documents: 3 classic papers, 2 CS231n slide decks, 1 Chinese article; an 85-page deck was rejected by the page limit in every arm), same generator (Claude Sonnet 5) and judge (GPT-5.6 Terra, rubric v2). Judge-only so far; the blind human check (`tools/eval/out/human-ab1/`) is pending.

## Arms

| Arm | Pipeline |
|---|---|
| **A** | plan → whole level per call → validate/repair (today) |
| **A+G** | A + **support gate**: per unit, a flash-tier check "is each marked answer right according to the cited passages?" → regenerate once → drop |
| **B** | **layered**: claims (+ quote/number/scope/entailment/conflict checks) → plan over claims → one call per unit with typed distractor mutations → deterministic checks → the same gate |

## Results (units, judge 1–5)

| Arm | Units | grounded | unambiguous | important | feedback | mechanic fit | mobile clarity | units with a wrong answer | **wrong answer parts** | answer leaks | USD / doc | level 1 (median s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 63 | 3.81 | 4.11 | 4.02 | 3.14 | 4.46 | 4.63 | 6/63 | 11/409 (**2.7 %**) | 15 | 0.87 | 153 |
| A+G | 63 | 4.06 | **4.57** | **4.17** | 3.38 | **4.73** | 4.60 | 3/63 | 3/418 (**0.7 %**) | 13 | 0.96 | 200 |
| B | 86 | **4.30** | **4.57** | 3.59 | **3.97** | 4.48 | 4.14 | 7/86 | 15/699 (2.1 %) | **42** | 2.08 | 177 |

B's numbers merge the main run (5/6 documents) and a rerun of the sixth after fixing a robustness bug (one empty check answer failed the job).

## Against the pre-registered rule

| Criterion (B vs A) | Result |
|---|---|
| grounded ≥ A + 0.5 | +0.49 — just short |
| verified wrong answers ≤ half of A | judge: 2.1 % vs 2.7 % — **not** halved (human check pending) |
| cost ≤ A | 2.4 × A — **fails** |

**Decision (ADR-015)**: **adopt the support gate as the default** (A+G: wrong answer parts 2.7 % → 0.7 %, +10 % cost, +~50 s to level 1). **Do not adopt the layered pipeline now**; keep it behind `A2G_PIPELINE=layered`.

## Why B did not win (what to fix before testing it again)

| Finding | Cause | Fix to try |
|---|---|---|
| Answer leaks 42 vs 13 | the unit writer knows each distractor's mutation and writes hints like "one card generalises beyond the Transformer" | hints must not name mutations or which part is wrong; deterministic check: no hint mentions a mutation kind or part id |
| Lower "important" (3.6) and "mobile clarity" (4.1) | atomic claims carry their full scope → wordy items about narrow facts | plan over claim *groups* (an idea = 2–4 claims); length budget per mechanic |
| 2.4 × cost | per-unit calls each resend chunk context; claim extraction + entailment add ~20 % | frames read units + claims instead of the whole document (29 % of B's cost, same in A); share chunk context per level |
| Robustness | one truncated or empty batch failed whole jobs (fixed: split-and-retry, fail-closed per batch) | — |

**What B did better**: feedback 3.97 vs 3.38. Typed distractors let the explanation name the misconception. That idea can be ported to the classic prompt on its own and A/B-tested cheaply.

## Next experiments (cheap, one variable each)

1. A+G + "name the misconception" feedback rule in `level.v1` (from B).
2. A+G + frames fed with units + cited chunks instead of the whole document (cost).
3. B v2 with the three fixes above, only if 1–2 leave a gap.

## Severity of answer leaks (owner question, 2026-11-19)

Split of the judge's leak flags: A 15 = 7 in hints + 8 visible; A+G 13 = 7 + 6; B 42 = 11 + **31**.

| Issue | Severity | Why | Policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wrong answer** | blocker | teaches something the document does not say; destroys trust | support gate (on by default); never ship |
| **Visible leak** (prompt / card / option / bin label / lead-in gives it away) | quality & difficulty | turns recall into matching — less learning (the effortful part *is* the value) and less challenge; teaches nothing false | scored, not blocked; allowed as gentle difficulty in level 1 and the Companion frame; not allowed in Case/boss items |
| **Leak in hints** | acceptable by design | the player asked for help; the engine records "correct with help" and does not raise mastery | no longer counted as a problem (rubric v3 separates it) |

The decision against B does not rest on leaks (its main gaps: 3 × A+G's wrong parts, 2.4 × cost); under this split B still has 31 visible leaks vs A+G's 6.
